Attest verifier

Checks an Attest signed copy on this device, with no Attest service: the document, every signer's passkey signature and entries, the signed state, and its anchor on a public blockchain.

Drop the signed PDF here, or click to choose it.
You can also drop attest-evidence.json together with original-document.pdf. Nothing is uploaded.

How this works

Every fingerprint is SHA-256. Each signature is a WebAuthn (passkey) assertion over the SHA-256 of the signer's exact statement. The signed state's fingerprint is a leaf of a Merkle tree whose root was written to the chain by a transaction anyone can read. The evidence file lists every step in plain words under checks, so any SHA-256 tool, WebAuthn library and Ethereum node can repeat them.
